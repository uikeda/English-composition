@E X68-3 | 例題68 解説3 | 出産、育児のため女性が休むことは昇進の面で著しく不利でした
= if women take several months off (in order) to have babies and raise them [take care of them], they find it extremely [very] difficult [they are less likely] to get promoted [get a promotion]
= if women are away from work (in order) to give birth or take care of their children, they have great difficulty getting a promotion
! 現状を考えれば used to (V) で過去の話にするのは無理があるので現在形。disadvantage なら women are at a great disadvantage in terms of promotion。take maternity [childcare] leave も可。take a rest は「ちょっと休む」で不可。
@E X68-4 | 例題68 解説4 | 今後は休んだあとの復職を容易にするとともに、…していかねばなりません
= It is absolutely necessary to make it easier for women to return to their jobs [to work again / to go back to work], and ...
= Now, we need [have] to make a great effort [to strive / to work hard / to do our best] to help women return to work, and also ...
! 「休んだあとの」は1つにまとめて文末に置けばよい。「今後は」は now / from now on / in (the) future。
@E X68-5 | 例題68 解説5 | 復職後業績を上げれば速やかに昇進できる体制をつくり上げて（いかねばなりません）
= establish [set up / create / build] a system in which they can get promoted rapidly [quickly / promptly / swiftly / easily / immediately] if they do a good job [do well at work] after they return to their jobs
= create a system that [which] enables [allows] them to get a rapid [quick / prompt / swift / immediate] promotion if they succeed (in their work) [if they achieve good results] after they start [begin] to work again
= help them get promoted as easily as men if they do a good job after they go back to their jobs [work]
! 「1つの体制」に定まることはないので the system in which ... は不可。「業績を上げる」は「よい仕事をする」「仕事で成功する」くらいで十分。

@E X69-2 | 例題69 解説2 | 人生に満足しているとか幸せな気持ちといった心の状態を表す
= describe how you feel when you are completely (entirely / totally) satisfied with your life or you are leading [living] a fulfilling life
= describe a state of mind such as being completely satisfied or being content with your life
= describe feelings of (a great deal of / full / deep / great) satisfaction [contentment] with your life or of (pure) joy
! 「心の状態」は how you feel と節で表現すれば簡単（a state of mind を知っていれば使う）。「～を表す」は describe がベスト（express / show も × ではない）。happiness の説明なので happy は使いたくない。satisfied だけでは「ギリギリ基準を満たしている」の意味になるので強意の副詞を足す。

@E P70-1 | 原則70-1 | 助動詞（could / might）を足して断定を避ける
= could / might を足す
! 日本語は受信者が状況を忖度して解釈するが、英語は発信者が責任を負う。100%断定してよい場合を除き、断定を避ける言葉を足す。
@S 例1 | 海外旅行は危険だ（→になりうる）
> Traveling abroad could be [× is] dangerous.
~ P70-1 : could be
@E P70-2 | 原則70-2 | generally / in general「一般に」などを足して断定を避ける
= generally / in general を足す
@S 例2 | 西洋人が握手をする状況で、日本人はお辞儀をする（→のが一般的だ）
> Generally, Japanese bow in situations in which Westerners would shake hands.
~ P70-2 : Generally
@E P70-3 | 原則70-3 | usually「普段は」を足して断定を避ける
